About TokerTravPrints Built by a Real Person, Not a Factory

This isn’t a print farm or a drop‑shipping operation. It’s a small, hands‑on 3D printing setup focused on making cool stuff the right way.

What This Is

TokerTravPrints started as a hobby and turned into a way to help people bring their ideas into the physical world. The focus is simple: clean PLA prints, honest limits, and results that look good when they’re done.

Everything is printed one job at a time. No mass production. No pretending a desktop printer is an industrial machine. If something makes sense to print, it gets printed well.

What This Is Not

This is not an industrial service. It’s not meant for heavy‑load, high‑stress, or safety‑critical parts. Prints are made in PLA and intended for novelties, toys, hobby projects, and display pieces.

There are no design services offered — files must be ready to print. That keeps things straightforward and avoids surprises for everyone involved.

How Printing Works Here

  • Material: PLA only
  • Max size: 10″ × 10″ × 10″
  • Layer heights from 0.20mm down to 0.06mm
  • Print‑only — bring your own file or choose an existing design

If your project fits within those limits, you’ll get a clean, carefully printed result.

Why Do It This Way?

Because not everything needs to be over‑engineered, upsold, or wrapped in buzzwords. Sometimes you just want a solid print from someone who actually cares about how it turns out.
