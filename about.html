About

Toker Trav’s Prints is a small, hands‑on 3D printing shop focused on functional, real‑world solutions — not mass‑produced plastic.

Built by a Real Person

This isn’t a print farm or an automated storefront. Every project is reviewed, adjusted, and printed by a human who actually cares how it’s used.

Function First

Strength, fit, and purpose come before aesthetics. If a part needs to survive real use, it’s designed with that in mind.

Problem‑Solving Mindset

Many projects start with “I can’t find this anywhere.” That’s usually where 3D printing makes the most sense.

Iterative by Nature

Prototypes, revisions, and improvements are part of the process. The goal is to get it right — not rush something out the door.

Transparent Pricing

You’ll know what something costs before it’s printed. No surprise fees, no vague estimates.

Have an Idea?

If you’re not sure how to describe it yet, that’s fine. Start the conversation and we’ll figure it out together.
