About the Maker

Why this exists, and how it’s different from big print services.

Built by a Real Person

Toker Trav’s Prints started the same way a lot of good ideas do — something broke, replacement parts were overpriced or unavailable, and “there has to be a better way” turned into a printer running late at night.

This isn’t mass production. It’s one person running well‑tuned machines, dialing in prints carefully, and making parts that are meant to actually be used.

Why Small‑Scale Matters

Attention to Detail

Prints are checked, tested, and adjusted — not rushed through a queue of hundreds.

Clear Communication

You talk to the person doing the work. No tickets. No support maze.

Honest Expectations

If something won’t work well as a print, you’ll hear that up front.

What This Shop Is (and Isn’t)

  • ✔ Functional parts, prototypes, and custom solutions
  • ✔ One‑offs and small batch runs
  • ✔ Collaborative problem solving
  • ✖ Mass‑produced novelty items
  • ✖ Cheapest‑possible prints at the expense of quality
